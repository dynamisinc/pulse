# Glossary

Pulse terms in plain language, in alphabetical order. Names in **bold** are the labels you see on
screen.

**After-action material.** See *Exercise report* and *Export posts*.

**AI engine.** The part of Pulse that drafts posts for personas while the exercise runs, so the
world reacts to what players do. Its drafts go to the *review queue*. Controllers control the engine
from the Controller Console. The engine bar shows **ENGINE · LIVE**, **ENGINE · SUGGEST-ONLY** or
**ENGINE · STOPPED**.

**Alert bar.** A band across the top of every player screen, standing in for an emergency alert
system. Alerts can be info, advisory or emergency, and they are always shown with words, not colour
alone. *Controllers cannot publish alerts to it yet.*

**Archived.** The final [exercise state](getting-started.md#exercise-states). An archived exercise
is closed out, read-only for staff, and cannot be reopened.

**Autonomy.** How much the AI engine may do without a person. With **Suggest**, every draft waits for
a controller. With **Delayed-auto**, each draft carries a countdown. If nobody acts before it runs
out, the draft is held for a controller. Pulse never publishes a draft just because nobody answered.

**Build.** The first exercise state. You are preparing content and players cannot get in.

**Card (sign-in card).** A printable card for one player, with their QR code, sign-in name and a line
to write today's word on. Print cards from **Participants > Print cards**. Sometimes called a join
card.

**Channel.** One part of the player world. Only the social network is available today. News, TV,
newspaper, wire, press room and weather are not available yet.

**Citizen persona.** The persona Pulse creates for a player who is not operating an organization
account. It is named after them, for example `@jane_smith`. The roster marks it **Auto citizen**.

**Completed.** The exercise state after **EndEx**. Players see an end-of-exercise page and nobody
can sign in.

**Compliance chrome.** The exercise markings on every player screen: the top and bottom banners and
the in-content **EXERCISE** watermark. At least one of them must be on. Set them under
**Exercise Settings > Compliance chrome**.

**Controller Console.** The controllers' screen for running the exercise. See the
[Controller guide](controller-guide.md).

**Day word.** See *Today's word*.

**EndEx.** The end of the exercise. A controller selects **End exercise** and types END to confirm.
The exercise moves to **Completed** and this cannot be undone.

**Evaluator Dashboard.** The evaluators' screen. *It is not on live data yet.*

**Exercise Management.** The screen where planners, org admins and controllers create exercises and
see all of the organization's exercises.

**Exercise report.** A printable record of the exercise: the scenario window, an at-a-glance summary,
activity charts, responses to injects, lookalike-account activity and every post. All times in it
are scenario time. Open it with **Exercise report** in the Controller Console, then use **Print /
Save as PDF**.

**Exercise Settings.** The planner's screen for configuring one exercise. The page is headed
**Exercise configuration**.

**Exercise state.** Where an exercise is in its life: Build, Staged, Live, Paused, Completed or
Archived. See [Exercise states](getting-started.md#exercise-states).

**Export posts.** A Controller Console button that downloads a spreadsheet of every post.

**Fake provider.** An offline stand-in for the AI engine's language model. It produces predictable
practice content and uses no live AI service. It is used for demonstrations and testing.

**Freeze.** See *Pause tiers*.

**Holding page.** The page that replaces the feed on every player screen while the world is frozen
or the exercise is paused. In fiction it reads "We'll be right back". Out of fiction it reads
"EXERCISE PAUSED". It clears by itself when the exercise resumes.

**Hostname.** The web address an exercise answers on. Each hostname is unique across Pulse.

**Inject.** A planned piece of scenario content. In Pulse, an inject is a post, or a burst of posts,
on the *run sheet* that a controller fires at a planned scenario time.

**Live.** The exercise state after **StartEx**. The scenario clock runs and scripted content fires.

**Live world.** The column of the Controller Console that shows the feed as it happens, with staff
tools such as **Take down**.

**Lookalike account.** An unverified persona made to resemble a verified one, for example a fake
utility account with a near-identical name and picture. Lookalikes train players to check before
they trust or share. They carry no warning mark, by design. See also *Verified account*.

**MSEL.** Master Scenario Events List: the exercise's planned events, built in Cadence. Pulse imports
the social-media rows of a Cadence MSEL export into the run sheet with **Import from Excel**.

**NPC.** Pulse calls these *personas*.

**Org admin.** A role that manages exercises for the whole organization through **Exercise
Management**.

**Organization persona.** A persona for an agency or organization, such as a county emergency
management office. A player can be given one to operate. The roster marks it **Org**.

**Participant.** A player in the exercise. Participants use the social network and never see a staff
screen. See the [Participant guide](participant-guide.md).

**Pause tiers.** The three levels of hold in the Controller Console:
**Pause injects** (the world keeps living), **Pause engine** (no new AI content) and **Freeze world**
(everything stops, the scenario clock stops, and players see the holding page). The staff header shows
INJECTS PAUSED, ENGINE PAUSED or WORLD FROZEN.

**Paused.** The exercise state in which the world is held and players see the holding page.

**Persona.** A fictional account in the exercise world, such as a resident, an agency, a reporter or a
lookalike. Controllers post as personas from the Controller Console. Each player posts as their own
persona.

**Personal link.** A player's own sign-in link. Tapping it, or scanning its QR code, signs them in
with nothing to type. Staff can copy, issue, reissue or revoke it on **Participants**.

**Pin.** A player's **Pin** saves a post to their own **Pinned** tab, privately. A controller can also
use **Pin for everyone** in the Controller Console, and that post is then marked **Pinned** for all
players.

**PIO.** A participant role for public information officers. For now it works the same as
participant.

**Practice mode (Practice / sandbox).** Marks an exercise as a rehearsal, so that its data is left out
of evaluation exports. Players never see it.

**Preview as participant.** A staff-header button that shows the real player feed, read-only, inside
a staff screen.

**QR code.** The square code on a sign-in card. Scanning it opens the player's personal link.

**Review queue.** The list of AI engine drafts waiting for a controller. For each draft, a controller
can **Approve**, **Veto**, **Edit** or **Re-roll** (ask for a new draft).

**Roster.** The list of everyone who can sign in to the exercise as a player, on the **Participants**
screen.

**Run sheet.** The Controller Console's ordered list of injects for the exercise, with each item's
planned scenario time and status.

**Scenario time.** The exercise's own clock. Players only ever see scenario time. See
[Scenario time](getting-started.md#scenario-time).

**Sign-in name.** What a player types, with today's word, to sign in. It is private and is never the
same as their public persona handle.

**Staged.** The exercise state in which players can sign in and the background world can run, but
the scenario has not started.

**StartEx.** The start of the exercise. A controller selects **Start exercise**, the exercise goes
**Live**, and the scenario clock starts.

**Takedown.** A controller's removal of a post from the live world with **Take down**. The post
disappears from player feeds.

**Today's word.** One word for the whole exercise, for one day, that players combine with their
sign-in name to sign in without their link. Pulse shows it to staff only once, when it is set. Also
called the day word.

**Verified account.** A persona that carries a verified mark. Players should learn to look for it,
and to notice when a lookalike does not have it.

**View-only code.** A shared code that lets observers sign in to read the feed. They cannot post,
reply, like or pin.

**White noise.** Background chatter: ordinary posts that fill the feed so that the scenario content
does not stand out too much. Controllers set it to **Off**, **Low**, **Medium** or **High**.

**World name.** The place name players see, for example "Metro Atlanta". Set it under **Exercise
Settings > Identity & schedule**.
