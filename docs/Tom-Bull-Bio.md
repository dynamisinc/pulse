# Tom Bull — Professional Bio

Tom Bull is the originator and product leader of ScenarioForge, the Dynamis suite of purpose-built exercise technology spanning the HSEEP lifecycle. The entire suite is his concept, and he holds formal P&L ownership alongside product and delivery leadership. He leads the Beat team producing AI-generated exercise media, and personally drove Cadence (HSEEP exercise management and conduct) and Pulse (simulated public-information environment) from business analysis and design through implementation, fielding, training, and iteration. A hands-on technologist working primarily in TypeScript and C# on React and Azure, Tom pairs engineering depth with front-line customer engagement — leading sales and marketing, responding to RFPs, and cultivating partnership and reseller opportunities. He also identifies new capabilities for the Dynamis flagship COBRA / C5 incident-management platform, keeping the exercise suite and real-world response offerings aligned across the emergency management, public safety, and homeland security markets.
