## Overview

Dynamis delivers HSEEP-consistent, end-to-end exercise programs for emergency management, public safety, and homeland security organizations. Our **ScenarioForge** suite equips every phase of the exercise lifecycle — design, conduct, evaluation, and improvement — with technology our own exercise professionals build and run. Buyers get facilitators who understand the tools and tools shaped by real exercise practice. ScenarioForge complements the Dynamis flagship COBRA / C5 incident-management platform: COBRA owns real-world response; ScenarioForge owns exercise and training.

## Core Capabilities

**Beat — AI exercise media studio.** AI-generated news broadcasts, B-roll, anchor-desk segments, images, and articles featuring a customer's actual venues, landmarks, and jurisdiction. Produced in days, not weeks, at a fraction of traditional video cost, and delivered as timed injects during play. Typical delivery: 5–10 videos per exercise.

**Cadence — HSEEP exercise management & conduct.** A conduct-first, offline-capable platform that replaces spreadsheet MSELs and group chats with structured, role-based control: real-time inject delivery on an exercise clock, dual-time tracking, the FEMA PrepToolkit inject lifecycle, and the full HSEEP Exercise Evaluation Guide (EEG) with P/S/M/U scoring and AAR/IP-ready export.

**Pulse — simulated public-information environment.** A simulated "internet" — social network, news outlets, press wire, weather service, and exercise portal — where PIOs and EM staff practice monitoring feeds, countering misinformation, and managing public sentiment. An adaptive content engine makes the simulated world react to what participants do and fail to do.

## Differentiators

- **HSEEP-native, all-hazards, tabletop through full-scale** — not a generic tool retrofitted to exercises.
